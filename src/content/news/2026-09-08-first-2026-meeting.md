Please join us for our 1st monthly meeting of the 26/27 Snowmobile Season this Thursday Sept 10th. Meeting will be held at Dirt Designs at 73 Laundromat Rd, Thornton, NH.

## Membership Vouchers

Membership is now open! SledNH reports this week that total state membership is up 13% to LY as of this week. Signing up early benefits the club financially and allows us to plan for future projects before the season starts. If you are planning on helping this fall with any trail work, you must be a current registered member for insurance. You can register for membership here : CNHSC Online Membership – SledNH

## Race into Winter October 9th, 10th & 11th

We are about a month away from the annual Grass Drags, which really kick off the season for the snowmobiling community here in NH and all over New England. Attached is a link with all information about the event. Link: https://slednh.com/spectators-at-nh-grass-drags/

## 2027 Super Raffle Tickets

Watch out for posts starting next week as we will begin our ticket sales for the Super Raffle. Which will be held on February 7th, 2027. Each donation for a $20 ticket is a contribution towards the club!

![Club news](/images/SuperRaffle.jpeg)

We are excited to be back at it. As the crisp air starts to set in, it’s time to get in the trails to get them ready for action. Look out for trail work parties starting soon!
